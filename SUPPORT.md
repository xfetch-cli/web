<h2 id="before-opening-an-issue" align="center">Before Opening an Issue</h2>

<ul>
  <li>For documentation errors: note the page URL, the language (<code>en</code>/<code>es</code>/<code>de</code>) and what the source repo says instead.</li>
  <li>For site bugs: include the browser, the page, and a screenshot when possible.</li>
  <li>Content changes must be mirrored in all three languages — mention the others when proposing a fix.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="response-expectations" align="center">Response Expectations</h2>

<p>
  Maintainers triage reports based on impact and reproducibility. Clear, minimal reproductions are resolved faster.
</p>
